# A brighter monochange, in its own colours

The website and the projects workspace now share the violet of the monochange logo instead of a borrowed teal. Pages sit on a soft lavender ground in the light theme and a deep aubergine in the dark theme, and the homepage opens on a rich ultraviolet band. Links, buttons and focus outlines stay easy to see in both themes.

The release preview on the homepage now shows each change by its colour as well as its label: a patch shows in mint, a minor in saffron and a major in coral. Packages that only release because they depend on the change stay violet, so you can see at a glance what you changed and what it pulled along. Status badges, notices and errors in the workspace use the same small set of colours, so they look the same wherever you see them.
